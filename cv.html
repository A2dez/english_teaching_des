About Des

English language teaching · Linguistics · Communication

Test booking link

I help people use English with greater confidence, clarity and control — whether they are preparing for university, developing their professional communication, or working on the finer points of spoken English.

I’m Dr Des Ryan, an English language teacher and linguist with extensive experience teaching international learners and university students. I hold a PhD in Linguistics from Trinity College Dublin and have taught English since 2007, working with learners from beginner to advanced level.

My approach combines practical teaching with a detailed understanding of how English works. I explain the patterns behind pronunciation, grammar and spelling, then help you apply them through structured practice and feedback.

What I can help you with

Spoken English and pronunciation

Work on clarity, intelligibility, rhythm and connected speech. Understand not just what to practise, but why particular sounds and patterns can be difficult.

Academic English

Develop presentations, seminar contributions, essays and research communication. Practise organising ideas and expressing complex points clearly.

Professional communication

Prepare for meetings, interviews, workplace conversations and presentations. Build language that is clear, appropriate and natural for your context.

Grammar, vocabulary and writing

Get clear explanations and targeted practice to improve accuracy, range and confidence in written and spoken English.

My doctoral research was supported by the Irish Research Council. It investigated the principles that shape English spelling, including the ways spelling represents pronunciation, distinguishes words and preserves relationships between related forms.

How I teach

I aim to make lessons clear, focused and collaborative. I explain the reasoning behind a language point, give you opportunities to practise it, and use feedback to identify what will help you progress next.

You do not need to know linguistic terminology to benefit from this approach. I use technical detail when it makes something clearer, and keep the focus on your goals: communicating more comfortably, accurately and effectively.

My background in linguistics, university teaching and speech technology means I can work both with the bigger picture and with specific questions about sounds, words, grammar or writing.

A little more about me

I’m based in Wicklow, Ireland. Alongside teaching and linguistics, I’m interested in cycling, travel writing and storytelling. I speak English, French and some Irish, and I value the intercultural awareness that comes from working with learners with different languages and experiences.
